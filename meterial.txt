PRODUCT
Docketa is an independent Solana community application presented as a public
register. It supports an overview, quests, token status, advisory governance,
treasury reads, and a field guide. Routes remain
#home / #quests / #stake / #governance / #treasury / #learn.

The Anchor community program records quest participation and advisory votes.
It does not mint or distribute tokens, implement token locks, or execute
treasury spending. No token mint or token symbol is configured.

INTERFACE STRUCTURE
An orbit: a header stage carries the brand seal, six route nodes placed on a
circle, the route title inside a circular core, and the deck (lede, primary
actions, stats or proof) hanging in the ring's lower aperture, with a floating
command dock pinned to the viewport corner. A focused panel overlaps the orbit's
bottom edge and holds the route body: a readout band, a colonnade of ledgers,
ballot panels, meter dials, statement rows, steps, checklist and FAQ, with the
marginal notes as a second column. The colophon is a page footer with a rim arc
and an oversized wordmark. On small screens the nodes leave the ring and become a
staggered two-column arc under the seal, and the panel collapses to one column.
The X series is eight standalone posts (problem, stakes, intro, mechanism, trust,
proof, usage, close) drawn in the same Orbit Ledger direction (black ground, one
signal red, teal current, Trebuchet display, tracked mono). Three posts carry a
1600x900 card; five stay text. No post carries a contract address, because no
token mint exists.
