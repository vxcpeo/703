PRODUCT
Clepsyra is an independent Solana community application presented as a public
register. It supports an overview, quests, token status, advisory governance,
treasury reads, and a field guide. Routes remain
#home / #quests / #stake / #governance / #treasury / #learn.

The Anchor community program records quest participation and advisory votes.
It does not mint or distribute tokens, implement token locks, or execute
treasury spending. No token mint or token symbol is configured.

VISUAL
The site is a light fintech surface: white ground (#ffffff), soft panel tint
(#f9f8fa), ink text (#0d0d0d), muted #6d6a70, hairline rules (#e9e4eb) and one
magenta signal (#e6006f, #c2005d for text-bearing fills). Display type is a
geometric sans (Avenir Next / Segoe UI stack) with tight tracking; numerals and
micro-labels are mono and tracked. Corners are soft (12/22/32px), elevation is a
low cool shadow, and the ground carries faint magenta radial washes.
INTERFACE STRUCTURE
A two-column split: a sticky 38% narrative column (brand line with the brass mark,
a numbered vertical index of the six sections, a console with cluster, re-read,
wallet and menu, and the colophon footer) beside a 62% scrolling evidence stage.
The stage opens every section with a large running head and meta chips, then
composes from ruled ledger sections: a four-cell readout panel, quest rows,
ballot rows with split tally bars, receipt-style statement rows, numbered steps
and an accordion. Cards are flat records with a top rule, not floating panels.
On small screens the narrative stacks above the stage and the index becomes a
horizontal scroll strip. The retired fixed left spine, floating command bar, dark
canvas and page-bottom footer are not used.
Twitter launch material consists of five posts: one brand statement, one
participation post, one advisory-vote post, one register read, and one guide
note. No post carries a contract address; no token mint exists.
